WEM codec — browser demo

Drop a .wav (signed-16 PCM) to encode it to WEM, or a .wem to decode it to raw f32 PCM — in the browser (WebAssembly, wasm-bindgen shell over the Rust kernel), then download the result.

Drop a .wav or a .wem here, or click to choose (the profile data is compiled into the wasm module; an encode's configuration is auto-selected from the WAV's geometry, and a decode's geometry is announced by the container itself)
